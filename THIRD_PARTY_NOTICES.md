## Formerly bundled (removed in v3.0.0)

v2.x vendored anime.js, bezier-easing, d3-ease, d3-interpolate, d3-shape, svg-path-properties,
ai-film-studio and motion-reel source trees. All of it was removed with the restructure, because the
skill now teaches the technique and the agent implements it per project. If you reinstall any engine
inside a project, check its licence at that moment and keep its notice in that project's
`deliverables/NOTICES.txt` (guidance: `references/implementation.md` §8).
